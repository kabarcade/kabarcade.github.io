BeatJutsu: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Short version: the developer collects no personal data and has no accounts or servers. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

BeatJutsu is made by an independent developer, Parth Kabaria. Contact: kabarcade@gmail.com.

What the developer collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics run by the developer and no game servers. Your levels, stars, scores, balls, coins, items and settings are saved only in a file on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow it), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Leaderboards (Apple Game Center)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores on a few leaderboards (Endless, the daily challenge, total stars and the boss levels) are sent to Apple's Game Center, which shows them to other players with your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; the developer never sees your Apple Account. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings at any time; the game then keeps your bests on your phone only.

Your choices

  • Tracking on iPhone: the game asks for permission before any tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and some US states): where required, a Google consent form asks for your choices before ads load. You can reopen it any time from Settings > Privacy options in the game.
  • Playing offline: the whole game works offline; no ads load and nothing is sent.

Age

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
