Common questions

The notes feel early or late. Open Settings > Calibrate and tap along with the clicks. Bluetooth headphones add delay, so calibrate again when you switch to them.

Do I need an account or internet? No. The game works offline and your progress is saved on your phone.

How does the Plinko machine work? Balls come from playing levels and are never sold. The odds of each crate are shown on the machine, and each ball's result is picked with exactly those odds.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Settings > Privacy options in the game, and iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
